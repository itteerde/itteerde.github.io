Reflections

By the lost Cleric Enona

“Aetherial Expanse?”

What is the “Aetherial Expanse?” I have been told is was either a god, having sacrificed themselves in ancient times, or a catastrophe, caused by ancient mortals, rather than nature, or the super-natural. I don't know. It rings wrong to me. Though, who am I to judge a world I know nothing about? However things might be here, I will have to figure it out, at last as far as it concerns me -- which given my predicament seems to be a lot of it all.

Who am I here? Who was I before? How did I get here? It is well known to the peoples of this realm, that about a century ago, two empires from different, but certainly mortal, realms discovered the Expanse. A century, about five generations, means that one cannot necessarily trust the story tellers' account of that history. Well, may that be true accounts or the history written by victors, the Kingdom of Ayris, and the Empire of Karel are what en large controlls the fate of the peoples here -- though not only Freeborne take liberties with their rule of law.

The historians here seem to agree on both of them having had their world changed revolutionary by their discoveries here -- that it is “aetherium” that allowed them to raise quickly to powers they could not have imagined where they came from.

Pirates

The natives, if one can call them that, when scholars claim that everything and -one flesh-and-blood are invasive species from the mortal realms, call the struggle between the explorers, collonizers -- “War of the Invaders”. And given the multitude of communities and crews having spawned from that war's defectors, and the stories of their iron rule, who would I be to jude those who reject it, and try to make their own future, on that fact alone, that is. There are certainly those who slaughter and plunder. Still, it seems freedom lies with those outlaws, and as long as there is a code, I guess I'll live with that better than being pressed into either warrying empires' service.

Loyality to the Karelagne

Why would anyone having been press-ganged into service would feel any loyality to the emperor, or the church that keeps declaring the justification of the mission of the barbarians, while both get rich, now not only on gold and silver, but aetherium, too? And I do not think I would have survived my first year under that damn captain and his sadist first mate!

And so, marked for 20 years forced labor on the Crown's vessels, now having defacted, there never can be a return home. Therefore who is to judge me on taking to the rum and looking for whatever joy, or what one might mistake for it for a while? Damn the hypocritical theologicans and inquisitors. Damn the lords all to happily convict those whom they point out to them -- as that makes them free labor. The clergy would claim there are no Karelagne slaves. Well, most of their sailors will see that differently.

Fortunately it has been established that everyone here is hot. So I'll keep finding hot girls to drink and roll with and if the piracy allows to fuck the Karelagne navy over every now and then all the better.
