Ancestry · Faun

Caprine Leap

You can leap anywhere within Close range as though you were using normal movement, allowing you to vault obstacles, jump across gaps, or scale barriers with ease.

Kick

When you succeed on an attack against a target within Melee range, you can mark a Stress to kick yourself off them, dealing an extra 2d6 damage and knocking back either yourself or the target to Very Close range.

Community · Freeborne

Unbound

Once per session, when you make an action roll with Fear, you can change it to a roll with Hope instead.

Class · Rogue

Rogue's Dodge

Spend 3 Hope to gain a +2 bonus to your Evasion until the next time an attack succeeds against you. Otherwise, this bonus lasts until your next rest.

Sneak Attack

When you succeed on an attack while Cloaked or while an ally is within Melee range of your target, add a number of d6s equal to your tier to your damage roll.

  • Level 1 -> Tier 1
  • Levels 2–4 -> Tier 2
  • Levels 5–7 -> Tier 3
  • Levels 8–10 -> Tier 4

Cloaked

Any time you would be Hidden, you are instead Cloaked. In addition to the benefits of the Hidden condition, while Cloaked you remain unseen if you are stationary when an adversary moves to where they would normally see you. After you make an attack or end a move within line of sight of an adversary, you are no longer Cloaked.

Subclass · Syndicate

Well-Connected

When you arrive in a prominent town or environment, you know somebody who calls this place home. Give them a name, note how you think they could be useful, and choose one fact from the following list:

  • They owe me a favor, but they’ll be hard to find.
  • They’re going to ask for something in exchange.
  • They’re always in a great deal of trouble.
  • We used to be together. It’s a long story.
  • We didn’t part on great terms.

Contacts Everywhere

Once per session, you can briefly call on a shady contact. Choose one of the following benefits and describe what brought them here to help you in this moment:

  • They provide 1 handful of gold, a unique tool, or a mundane object that the situation requires.
  • On your next action roll, their help provides a +3 bonus to the result of your Hope or Fear Die.
  • The next time you deal damage, they snipe from the shadows, adding 2d8 to your damage roll.

Reliable Backup

You can use your “Contacts Everywhere” feature three times per session. The following options are added to the list of benefits you can choose from when you use that feature:

  • When you mark 1 or more Hit Points, they can rush out to shield you, reducing the Hit Points marked by 1.
  • When you make a Presence Roll in conversation, they back you up. You can roll a d20 as your Hope Die.

Other

Helmsperson

A ship is lost without a steady hand on the wheel. A good helmsperson must be calm under pressure and be able to guide their vessel through danger with little margin for error.

Base Helmsperson Feature (Ship Combat). Once per round during ship combat, you can spend a Hope to move your ship 1 square for free during the Movement Phase. You can do this a number of times equal to your helmsperson rank. You can refresh all uses of this ability with a short rest.

Expanded Features

Rank 2. Boon. Choose one boon from the boon list.

Rank 3. Dramatic Maneuver (Voyage). When you fail to avoid an environmental hazard during a pursuit, you can spend a Hope to reroll your action or reaction roll, and you must take the new result. Once you use this feature, you must use a downtime move while docked at a port to regain its use.

Rank 4.Boon. Choose one boon from the boon list.

Rank 5. Handles Like a Dream (Mastery). When at the helm of a Medium or Huge ship at the start of combat, you can improve your ship's mobility to that of the next smaller ship (a Huge ship becomes balanced, while a Medium ship becomes high.) This benefit lasts until the end of ship combat, and you must use a downtime move while docked at a port to regain its use. While at the helm of a small ship, there is no longer a limit to the number of times you can use your base helmsperson feature.

Helmsperson's Achievements

Helmspersons earn their promotions by performing ship maneuvers and managing the ship's speed. Some suggestions for achievements as a helmsperson are:

  • Catch or outrun another ship during a pursuit.
  • Defeat another ship in combat after successfully “Crossing the T.”
  • Move the ship 3 more squares during a combat turn than is granted by its base speed.

Boon: Sharp Spotter

This character has advantage when rolling Instinct action rolls to notice hidden hazards beneath the aether or ships trying to approach stealthily, and can spend a point of Hope prior to rolling such an action roll to automatically spot another ship on the horizon before they spot you. If an adversary on an opposing ship possesses this boon and spends a point of Fear on it, then the two spotters would simultaneously see each other's ships.

Rations

This item represents enough rations to feed a character for a few days. So long as a character has this item on them when they travel, they do not suffer from the effects of hunger or thirst. If a character does not have this item on their person during a long rest taken as part of a travel narrative, they may not take the clear stress downtime action. Generally speaking, a character does not need to carry multiple uses of this item. Each item represents multiple servings of foodstuff. After a long rest, however, a GM may spend a Fear in order to force a character to make an Instinct Roll with a difficulty based on the terrain. On a failure, the character runs out of rations and must consume this item.

Biography

She/Her Age 35

Background

What did you get caught doing that got you exiled from your home community? I seemingly betrayed my community to try and depose the despot who took over by working for him. I became involved in various activities at the behest of the Stardogs in order to gain repute, which caught the attention of the ambitious Prince-Captain Tammon d’Ayre, the same man who killed my father. While I still wish to free my community, I will always be looked at as a traitor for my supposed betrayal.

You used to have a different life, but you’ve tried to leave it behind. Who from your past is still chasing you? While not actively “chased”, I am still technically under the thumb of Prince-Captain Tammon’s whim. While I may not be a priority, I will eventually need to report back to him and the Stardogs or else my home may pay the price I’m so desperately trying to protect them from.

Who from your past were you most sad to say goodbye to? My adoptive father, Don Felipe Doboro, an ex-Privateer who worked directly with King Pietro Jaconno and the Princess Naelana was brutally killed in front of me by the Stardogs themselves. In his final moments, all he can do was look at me with his kind eyes and offer me the warmth that he unfailingly provided.

What secret do you know about the Shipgrave treasure? The treasure was laid to rest here by the great merfolk pirate Amil Markhoff, captain of the lost ship Legend.

How did you learn this secret? From one of my many contacts throughout the expanse, who I sought out after learning that Tammon and his Stardogs would love the get their hands on this treasure. If I can get to it first, I can make it look like a dead end, when in reality I make out with so much more to help my people.

Connections

What did I recently convince Wilhelm to do that got us both in trouble? After a particularly bad turn that resulted in the deaths of a few of our fellow crewmen, I convinced Wilhelm to go out for a night of drinks to get our minds off things, let’s just say things got a wee bit out of hand and there were more than a few cracked heads and damaged furniture before the night was done.

What have I discovered about Wilhelm's past that I hold secret from the others? When Wilhelm was younger, Wilhelm was out on the seas with his sister and was beset by pirates. As he fought to defend them, he lost himself in a rage and when he finally snapped out of it he was the only one left standing. Whether his sister fell to the pirates or by his own hand he never knew and was never brave enough to try and find out.

Who does Wilhelm know from my past, and how have they influenced his feelings about me? Wilhelm knew Nikki’s father, Don Felipe Deboro. Wilhelm respected Deboro's staunch loyalty to King Pitro Jaconno and Princess Naelana: even in the face of dire threats and death from Prince Tammon, Doboro did not betray them. In Nikki, Wilhelm sees a lot of Nikki's father and thus feels more comfortable about trusting her with secrets that he would not share with any other.
