Backstory

A terrible creature hurt your community, and you’ve vowed to hunt them down. What are they, and what unique trail or sign do they leave behind? A pair of genies, one of fire and the other of water, turned my life into a living hell. Ever since the Raven Queen saved me from them, they try torment me and those closest to me. The only sign they are near is a pair of stars that move along the night sky, usually followed by a terrible lightning storm.

Your first kill almost killed you, too. What was it, and what part of you was never the same after that event? A woman by the name of Eloysia was the first kill that I truly felt changed me. She had hired me and a group of adventurers to escort her through an old dungeon in search of a relic. She betrayed us and caused the death of good allies. A little while later I killed her, but she was brought back by magic. Then a few encounters later I managed to kill her and steal her corpse away from those who would bring her back. As a reward for ‘sacrificing’ her, I was given a golden card which could use to summon her tormented soul to fight by my side. Ever since then I couldn't look at myself in the mirror. I've since stopped using it, and it seems to have lost its magic.

You’ve traveled many dangerous lands, but what is the one place you refuse to go? There are two, actually. The elemental plane of water and the elemental plane of fire. Should I step onto either of those planes, I fear I may run into the genie twins who stole years of my life and who tormented me. Here they can be dealt with, but there I doubt I would survive.

What secret do you know about the Shipgrave treasure? The treasure is aetherium worth hundreds of gold pieces, and it supposedly possesses the ability to charm aetherbeasts.

How did you learn this secret? My former captain kept detailed reports, nearly ancient, from when explorers first began to push into the Aethersea. I've read them. I've memorized them. If I can find them and tame one, I might be able to use it to rid myself of those genies once and for all.

For the GM’s eyes. The map she sold was a forgery that still hunts her.
