Ancestry · Faerie/Astral Emergent

Luckbender

Once per session, after you or a willing ally within Close range makes an action roll, you can spend 3 Hope to reroll the Duality Dice.

Aetherdip

You do not suffer the effects of aetherpoisoning. Instead, when you come into contact with aether, it tries to draw you back into its embrace. When you end any Move in contact with aether you must make a Presence reaction roll difficulty 10 or take 1d4 direct magic damage.

While you are in contact with the Aether, you may attempt to draw aether into yourself: make a Presence Roll; if you roll with Hope, you clear one Hit Point; if you roll with Fear, you take 1d4 direct magic damage. You may spend three points of Hope to avoid the action roll and simply clear one Hit Point. If you take this action during your Move, you do not suffer the normal effects for ending your Move in contact with aether. Alternatively, you may spend 3 points of Hope to become immune to the effects of aether for one scene. If you choose to do so, you cannot use aether to heal yourself during that scene. If you spend the majority of that scene in aether, you must mark 2d4 points of Stress at the end of that scene.

Community · Reborne

Found Family

Once per rest, you can spend a Hope to use an ally's Experience as if it were your own. When you do so, describe how your time with that ally prepared you for this moment.

When you join a new community or rediscover your old one, you can permanently trade this card for that one instead.

Class · Witch

Witch's Charm

When you or an ally within Far range fails an action roll, you can spend 3 Hope to change it into a success with Fear instead.

Hex

Mark a Stress to temporarily Hex a target within Far range. While Hexed, the target gains a penalty to their damage rolls and Difficulty equal to your tier. The maximum number of creatures you can Hex at one time is equal to your Spellcast trait.

Commune

Once per long rest during a moment of calm, you can commune with an ancestor, a deity, a spirit, or an otherworldly being. Ask them a question, then roll a number of d6s equal to your Spellcast trait. Choose one of the results and reference the chart below for the effect.

  • 1–3: You taste a flavor, smell a scent, or feel a sensation relevant to the answer.
  • 4–5: You hear sounds or see a vision relevant to the answer.
  • 6: You psychically experience a scene relevant to the answer as if you were there.

Subclass · Hedge

Herbal Remedies

When you or an ally in the scene clears 1 or more Hit Points or Stress as the result of using a consumable, increase the number cleared by 1.

Enchanted Talisman

Once per rest, you can imbue a small item with your protective essence. Spend any number of Hope to place an equal number of tokens on this card. When the person holding the talisman takes damage, spend a token to reduce the number of Hit Points they mark by one. Clear all tokens from this card when you take a rest.

Walk Between Worlds

During a moment of calm, make a Spellcast Roll (13). Once per rest on a success, you can mark a Stress to step beyond the veil of death and converse with any nearby spirits. Place a number of tokens equal to your Spellcast trait on this card and remove one each time a spirit answers a question. When the last token is removed or at the end of the scene, you return to the Mortal Realm in the same spot you left it.

Vexing Malison

You have advantage on attacks against Hexed creatures.

Circle of Power

Once per rest, mark a circle on the ground outlining a Very Close area around you, and place a number of tokens equal to your Spellcast trait on this card. While within this circle, you and your allies gain a +2 bonus to damage thresholds, attack rolls, and Evasion. Remove a token each time you or an ally within the circle makes an action roll or evades an attack. This spell lasts until the last token is removed or you exit the circle.

Other

Navigator

A good navigator is worth their weight in gold, as charting a reliable course through the Aetherial Expanse requires years of experience and a good working knowledge of current wind routes.

Navigators with magical talents are called Wayfinders, and they can use their spellcasting trait instead of Instinct when making Navigate action rolls for their ship.

Base Navigator Feature (Voyage). By spending a Hope, you can reroll a failed Navigate action roll made for your ship. You can do this a number of times equal to your navigator rank, but no more than once per travel check (see Travel). You can regain all uses of this feature with a downtime move used while your ship is docked at a port.

Expanded Features

Rank 2. Boon. Choose one boon from the boon list.

Rank 3. Planned Momentum (Ship Combat). During ship combat, when your ship is in irons, you can spend a Hope to move your ship 2 squares instead of 1. You can use this feature once per Short Rest.

Rank 4. Boon. Choose one boon from the boon list.

Rank 5. Aethersense (Mastery). If you would roll on a Travel Complication table, you can spend a Hope to roll twice and encounter the more beneficial complication. Alternatively, when you succeed on a Navigate action roll during a journey, you may choose to still encounter one of the ships from the Travel Complication table. The GM determines the type of ship. Regardless of which option of this feature you use, you must use a downtime move while docked at a port to regain it.

Navigator's Achievements

Navigators earn their promotions through skilled navigation – including locating important landmarks and other ships. Some suggestions for achievements as a navigator are:

  • Complete a multiple-leg journey without any navigation-related complications.
  • Track down a ship you're searching for while at sea.
  • Discover a lost or mythical location.

Boon: Never Say Die Reaction

Once per long rest, when a crew member you can see on the same ship or within Close range marks their last Hit Point slot, you can spend a point of Hope to choose to have them drop to 1 fewer marked Hit Points than their maximum number of Hit Point slots instead.

Reborn

When playing an astral emergent character, you should choose the first trait of another Ancestry whose body type your character resembles. This ties you to the creature who died beneath the aetherial waves. Unlike the body of your origin, however, you do not require air, food, or drink, and when you sleep or fall unconscious, your form flickers and wavers, becoming translucent and revealing the constellation of lights that make up your bones.

Consumables

Rations

This item represents enough rations to feed a character for a few days. So long as a character has this item on them when they travel, they do not suffer from the effects of hunger or thirst. If a character does not have this item on their person during a long rest taken as part of a travel narrative, they may not take the clear stress downtime action. Generally speaking, a character does not need to carry multiple uses of this item. Each item represents multiple servings of foodstuff. After a long rest, however, a GM may spend a Fear in order to force a character to make an Instinct Roll with a difficulty based on the terrain. On a failure, the character runs out of rations and must consume this item.

Tektites resemble obsidian, and can be thrown as an improvised weapon (Very Close range) that deals proficiency d4 slashing damage. The tektite shatters when it strikes anything solid.
