Session 12: The Maw at Lantern Reef

Logged by Tobias Fennick Quill, third day of the Gale Moon

The reef lights came on at dusk, one by one, like a town waking up beneath the clouds. Nobody aboard said what we were all thinking: that lanterns do not light themselves.

“If it hums, don’t touch it. If it sings, don’t answer. If it does both, run.”

Maru, on salvage

We dropped anchor at the edge of the glow and sent Wren aloft. She came down faster than she went up.

Below the reef, the cloud-floor parts. Something vast turns over in the dark, and a single pale eye the size of a ship’s wheel catches the lantern light and holds it.

Fear move

The Maw exhales ether-fog. Everyone marks a Stress, and the Albatross drifts one range band closer.

Hope moment

Isolde lights the signal lamp and the crew rallies. Each player gains a Hope.
