Session 11: Celsian Athenaeum

Played 2026-01-31In-world: ?

Arrival

The group reaches the, well one, real in-the-walls gate to the Celsian Athenaeum. Commander Kieran Tolas seems to be considering telling/ asking the group something. Maybe in time the group's status might reach a level with more areas accessible.

The group is housed where some recent visitors have been housed, too. It seems to be their place to look in people, too.

Sir Darren Thule, Elf, seems to be running the quarters. Stairs upwards are of limits.

Crane

Lynyxia helps workers to fix the crane they used to get the traded goods into the Celsian Athenaeum with, and one of the dwarves offers to have Tamsin “Stitch” Corvale craft something (armor) for her.

Marwen

Marwen, the gravedigger, catches the eye of Holly. He and Vaelin talk to her. Something about Vaelin made her engage the group. Something about her gives Vaelin goose bumbs, makes him uneasy about her. He feels that there is something like the Corrupted Saint in the crypts below the Celsian Athenaeum.
