Session 3: Essenburg Council

Played 2025-11-02In-world: ?

Council Meeting

Right away, when reaching Essenburg, the group calls for an emergency meeting of the village council. Gial informs the Council of Essenburg:

Honorable Councilmembers of Essenburg. May I address the Council?

Herr Bürgermeister, with deep regret I have to report the failure, only just short of total failure, of the expedition. I'll be brief, as you'll see, the time is pressing. Therefore, with urgency, six points:

  1. Standing before you are the only three survivors. The others killed in action, except the Ribbit, [unreadable], to be precise.
  2. After Innovindil made a deal with a people of Giants, who had taken the food, we discovered another, though corrupt, source of protection against the Umbra: a Fallen Saint, the Giants were making sacrifices to.
  3. For returning the food Innovindil made a deal with the Giants that they, their crops failing, could live in Essenburg. I know, I know. Lets get through the remainder of the points first, please.
  4. It is unknown if the chasm has anything to do with the Giants' crop failure, the saint -- or not at all. When we investigated Innovindil set free the Corrupted Saint, and it started killing most of us, and turned on the Giants.
  5. The food was stored at the site of the Corrupted Saint, and is truly gone now, while the murderous Corrupted Saint roams free.
  6. The Giants are refugees now, and will be at the gates soon -- without the food, but likely to blame us, and expect us to let them in.

There is no way around it. It is a disaster. For what should not be blamed on the dead I take full responsibility.

Being available for all your questions, and subjugating myself to whatever measures the council will decide on, is the least I can do.

Thank you for your patience, honorable Council members, Herr Bürgermeister.

First Blood

After the briefing, the council having sent the group on a mission to get a Branch, not yet knowing where from, the town militia's alarm is sounded. The militia has shot one of the Formorians. Jotan is dead.

Gial urges Alred Tetzlaft to issue orders stopping the fighting.
