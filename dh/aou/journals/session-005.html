Session 5: To the Atheneaum

Played 2025-11-28In-world: ?

Plan

Get though the woods to the shore, and build a raft. Bring personal floatation aids, axes, rope, extra clothing, blankets, and a small water tight barrel or two.

Over the bridge to the ridgeline overseeing the forest and path to the Tower Ruin, where the crossing is most narrow. The Toer is the pieces of the Statue of the Lady, that once greeted the ships arriving at Amber Reach.

Woods

After an encounter with Umbra-touched wolves and Rose-beasts, the group arrives at the shore, where they are attacked by Dryads or something.
