Session 2: Corrupted Saint

Played 2025-10-18In-world: ?

Opening the sarcophagus

Innovindil decides to open the sarcophagus, but cannot push the lid open against some force field. Asking the group to help, with Gial they manage to push open its lid. The Corrupted Saint ist set free and attacks. Talokai sacrifices herself in a Blaze of Glory, Holly Risks it All, and only he, Gial, and Lynyxia make it out alive and back to Essenburg.

Corruption

It seems the local corruption is different from the Umbra, probably about the chasm, maybe something to be learned from the bell that is suspected to have protected the village, but might have corrupted their priest. To be investigated in the morning.

Gial browses the corrupted priest's book once it turned out the sarcophagus is powering their pyre, which in turn does feed the bell.
