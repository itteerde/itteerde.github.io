Session 4: Fomorian Truce

Played 2025-11-08In-world: ?

Diplomacy

Alred Tetzlaft orders to only shoot if attacked, and the group walks out to the Fomorions, in order to manage their arrival.

The Fomorians are not to be reasoned with, not even accepting to wait with attacking the village longer than a day, allowing the group to look for the Branch.

The group gets a Fomorian guide and three days to kill the Corrupted Saint. Otmeck informs the Council of Essenburg. The group rest inside the Fomorian camp.

On the way back to the Fomorian village the group is attacked by giant scorpions and defeats them.
